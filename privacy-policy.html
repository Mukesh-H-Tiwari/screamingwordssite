Screaming Words Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This policy focuses on the Screaming Words mobile game. This website is a static information page with no advertising or analytics scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information when you visit; see GitHub's Privacy Statement .

This policy describes how the Screaming Words spelling game (package name com.bytecode.screamingwords) handles information. The game is published by Bytecode; developer contact: bytecode.creation@gmail.com. It is designed for children ages 5 and under and ages 6–8, with parent or guardian supervision. Bytecode is the publisher; Mukesh Tiwari is the developer contact for this policy.

Information the game stores on your device

The game stores spelling-game progress and sound, music, and speech preferences locally on your device. This information is used to remember progress and settings. The game does not use an account and does not send this local progress to a Bytecode server.

You can reset game progress in the game settings. Resetting progress does not reset sound, music, or speech preferences. Clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the game removes locally stored game data. A later Google Play purchase restore can make eligible purchases available again.

Google Play purchases

Optional chapter, pack, and full-game unlocks are one-time purchases processed through Google Play Billing. The game asks Google Play for available product information and whether recognized products are owned, so it can display Play-provided prices and unlock purchased content. Checkout, payment credentials, and payment processing are handled by Google Play, not by Bytecode. Google may process purchase, account, device, and transaction information under its own terms and privacy disclosures. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms and purchase help .

A purchase is not treated as complete merely because checkout was opened. The game waits for Google Play to report a completed purchase. Purchase options are shown behind a parent-facing screen. Parents and guardians should also use Google Play's family and purchase authentication controls on the device.

Services used by the game

  • Google Fonts: The game can request the Fredoka font from Google's font delivery service at fonts.gstatic.com. Google receives the network request, including the device's IP address and technical request information needed to deliver the font. The game does not use this request for advertising or profiling. Review Google's Google Fonts privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Text-to-speech: When speech is enabled, the game passes words or sentences to the text-to-speech engine installed and selected on the device. That engine may process the text on-device or remotely, depending on the provider and device settings. The game does not record the child's voice and does not request microphone access. The engine provider's privacy terms apply to its processing.
  • Google Play Billing: Google Play handles checkout and reports product ownership to the game as described above.

Advertising, analytics, and communications

The current game does not display advertising, include an analytics service, provide chat or social sharing, or ask a child to create an account. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or phone permissions. If these features or services change, this policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated before release.

Children and family privacy

The game is intended for the child age groups listed above. It is designed not to ask children for names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, photographs, or other direct contact information. Technical information may nevertheless be processed by Google services when fonts are requested or purchases are made, and by the device's selected text-to-speech provider as described above.

Parents or guardians may contact Bytecode with questions or privacy requests. Please do not include a child's sensitive information in a support email unless it is necessary to explain the request.

Sharing, retention, and security

Bytecode does not operate a game account or cloud database for this game, and does not sell children's personal information. Local game data remains in the application's storage on the device until it is reset, the application storage is cleared, or the game is uninstalled. Google Play, Google Fonts, and the device's text-to-speech provider handle information as described above under their respective terms.

Support correspondence sent to the contact address is used to respond to the request and is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for that purpose or to meet legal obligations. The game does not transmit locally stored progress to Bytecode. Network requests for Google services use HTTPS. The device operating system's application sandbox protects local app storage; do not treat the game as a backup service.

Parents' requests and contact

A parent or guardian can ask questions, request help with information sent to Bytecode, or raise a privacy concern by emailing bytecode.creation@gmail.com. Bytecode does not have a server-side child profile or game-progress database to retrieve or delete. Google Play purchase records are managed by Google Play and are associated with the user's Google account; contact Google for requests concerning those records.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be revised if the game's data practices or services change. The effective date above will be updated when a revision is published. Material changes will also be reflected in the relevant Google Play disclosures.
